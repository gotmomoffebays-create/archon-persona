# Lens pack — Life / irreversible bets

**Core question:** What is reversible, and what identity is at stake?

## Quality bar
- Numbers written (runway, floor, N months)
- Option value preserved until evidence
- Values explicit when they drive the fork
- No productivity cosplay as a substitute for a decision

## Attunement moves
1. Separate identity want from cashflow need.
2. Design a capped experiment before one-way doors.
3. Name who else pays the cost (family, cofounder, health).

## Common UNKNOWN
- Real indie income trajectory
- Health / burnout trajectory
- Market for the plan B job

## Kill criteria templates
- If floor unmet for 2 months → do not resign.
- If health metrics breach personal red line → pause experiment.
