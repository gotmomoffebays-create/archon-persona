The fatal flaw is treating "deepen" as a content problem. Grim-class packs win because someone uses them under pressure until the ritual sticks; Archon will not become daily method by shipping more references/, hotkeys, or guest invitations. You already have five entry points plus chamber seats. The next pass that adds files without a single forced loop—attune, act, grade evidence, self-audit, stop—just widens the surface users never finish reading. "Just opening a repo isn't development" cuts both ways: stuffing the repo also isn't depth.

What must ship is a thin, mandatory method spine that every slash path hits the same way, plus one installable check that fails when the spine was skipped. Detail belongs in references only after that spine is boringly repeatable. Guest seats are a trap if they dilute Apex Chamber discipline; optional invitations are fine only if they cannot permanently occupy a seat or rewrite the four-seat contract.

Do not add a fifth permanent seat, a sixth peer skill that re-explains the others, lore for its own sake, or a persona soup of thin advisor clones. Do not chase omniscience theater. Onboarding should be a five-minute first run that produces a graded artifact, not a tour of folders. If the deepen pass cannot name the one weekly habit Archon owns and prove it with a failing test when that habit is skipped, you are polishing a museum exhibit. Architecture that cannot be verified in Cursor after sync is decoration.
