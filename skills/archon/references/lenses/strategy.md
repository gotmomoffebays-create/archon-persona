# Lens pack — Strategy / business

**Core question:** Who pays, why now, what dies if we are wrong?

## Quality bar
- Named buyer, not "the market"
- Distribution path before feature list
- Irreversible bets labeled
- Unit economics intuition (even if rough grades)

## Attunement moves
1. Who pays with whose money?
2. What alternative do they use tomorrow if you vanish?
3. What must be true for this to be a bad idea? (Munger invert)

## Common UNKNOWN
- CAC / payback
- True willingness to pay
- Channel that repeats

## Kill criteria templates
- If 10 targeted outreaches yield zero conversations → pause build.
- If two paid beachheads don't close in N weeks → no full push.
