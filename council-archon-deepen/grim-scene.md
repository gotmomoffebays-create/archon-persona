# Grim Council — Archon deepen (scene excerpt)

The chamber’s green light caught the edge of a closed atlas that was not Grimoire’s.

"You want a second cult," Grimoire said, amused and unkind. "I already run one. Build a **method** that survives without applause."

Abathur’s filaments tightened. "Specimen: thin SKILL, fat ego. Evolve the **loop** — attune, grade, audit — until the system rejects ungraded finality. Compound that. Do not spawn twelve soft organs."

Gizmo hopped onto the atlas and nearly tipped the ink. "Ooh, fifth seat! Sixth! Seventh with a hat!" He paused. "…or don’t. Hats are how soup starts."

Helm from the merchants’ bench did not stand. "Ship install that a stranger finishes in five minutes. Brand that is not Grim fanfic. Then stop spending."

Quill scratched once. "Record the standard: four seats. Guests expire. Pages earn ink."

The Narrator let the room settle. Outside Grim’s spellbook, Archon’s circlet waited — a sentence, not a crown.
